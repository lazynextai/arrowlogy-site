Refund Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Beta — pending counsel review

The beta is free. There is no checkout on this site, we take no payments, and no card is required during the beta — so there is nothing to refund today. The sections below describe how refunds and cancellation will work if and when paid plans ship.

On this page

  1. Right now: beta is free
  2. When paid plans ship
  3. Cancelling a subscription
  4. How to request a refund
  5. Your statutory rights
  6. Contact

1. Right now: beta is free

The Arrowlogy private beta costs nothing. No card, no payment details, no trial that converts into a charge. You cannot be billed during the beta, and if your access ends you have lost nothing financially.

Because there is no payment, there is no refund to give. If you see anything on this site that looks like a checkout, or anyone asking you to pay for Arrowlogy, please email hello@arrowlogy.com before you pay — it is not us.

2. When paid plans ship

We plan a free tier plus a paid Pro plan for heavier use once the product is ready. Nothing is for sale today and no price is final. When paid plans do ship, this policy will be completed before anyone is charged, and it will cover at least:

  • the refund window and the conditions attached to it;
  • how unused processing allowances are treated on cancellation;
  • how taxes, currency conversion and payment-processor fees affect a refund;
  • the rules for failed jobs and service outages; and
  • how to cancel and what happens on your billing date.

We will not convert a beta account to a paid plan without your explicit agreement, and we will not take a payment without showing these terms at checkout.

3. Cancelling a subscription

Once subscriptions exist, cancelling will stop future charges. Access normally continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, because you have paid for that time. Cancellation will be available from your account settings and, failing that, by email — we will not require a phone call.

Cancelling is not the same as deleting your account. You can do both; deleting an account removes your project data under the retention rules in our Privacy Policy.

4. How to request a refund

During the beta, there is nothing to refund, but you can always email us. When paid plans exist, the route will be the same one: email hello@arrowlogy.com with the subject “Refund”, the email address on the account, and the date and amount of the charge.

We will acknowledge your request and aim to decide within 14 days. Approved refunds will be sent back to the original payment method; how fast it appears depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy reduces rights you have under mandatory consumer law where you live, including any right to cancel a distance purchase within a cooling-off period. Where such a right applies and conflicts with this policy, your statutory right wins.

This policy is written plainly and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Counsel will finalise the wording, the cooling-off treatment, and the tax handling before any charge is taken.

6. Contact

Billing or refund questions: hello@arrowlogy.com.
