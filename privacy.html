Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Beta — pending counsel review

The short version. We collect what we need to run a video editor: your account details, the media you upload, and basic technical logs. Your media is processed in the cloud and deleted within 24 hours of a job finishing. We never sell your data and we do not use your content to train models. This policy is written plainly and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Who we are

Arrowlogy (“we”, “us”) runs the Arrowlogy browser-based video editor and this website at arrowlogy.com. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at hello@arrowlogy.com.

The Service is in private beta and the editor is not publicly available yet. This policy describes how the beta works and the retention rules we are committing to for it.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your email address, a password (stored hashed), and a display name if you set one.
  • Google sign-in details — if you choose Google sign-in, we receive your email address, name and profile identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your content — the video, audio, images, captions and project data you upload or create, plus the exports we render for you.
  • Messages you send us — beta requests, support emails and feedback, including anything you attach.

Information we collect automatically

  • Technical and security data — IP address, browser and operating system, request timestamps, and error and security logs.
  • Usage data — pages viewed on this site, aggregate traffic statistics, and which features or job types are used in the editor.
  • Job records — that a job ran, its type, duration, status and size, so we can debug failures and enforce limits.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for payment details during the beta — there is no checkout and no card is required.
  • We do not collect special-category data on purpose. Do not upload medical, health, patient, biometric-identification or government-ID content; it is prohibited by our Acceptable Use Policy.

3. Why we collect it

We use personal data only for these purposes:

  • To provide the Service — authenticate you, store and process your uploads, run jobs, and return your exports.
  • To keep it working and safe — debugging, abuse prevention, fraud and malware checks, rate limiting, and enforcing our terms.
  • To communicate — sending your beta invite, security notices, and replies to your emails. Marketing email is opt-in only.
  • To improve the product — understanding aggregate usage and failure patterns. We use aggregated and de-identified signals, not your footage, to do this.
  • To comply with the law — responding to valid legal requests and meeting our record-keeping duties.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you (running the Service), our legitimate interests (security, debugging, improving the product), consent (optional marketing email), and legal obligation where applicable.

We do not use your content to train machine-learning models, and we do not sell your content or personal data to anyone.

4. Cookies and analytics

The editor needs a small number of cookies or local-storage entries to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. Those are strictly necessary.

This marketing site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused, cookie-free traffic measurement tool. It reports aggregate page views and performance, not individual profiles. It excludes visitors in the EU by default unless consent is in place, which means EU traffic may be undercounted. We do not run advertising trackers or sell analytics data.

5. How long we keep it

DataRetention
Uploaded source media and intermediate files Deleted within 24 hours of the job finishing, or sooner
Rendered exports Held only long enough for you to download them, then deleted — within 24 hours by default
Project metadata (timeline, settings) While your account exists
Account details While your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request
Job and security logs Up to 90 days, unless needed longer for an active security or abuse investigation
Emails you send us Up to 24 months, so we can follow up on beta and support threads

The 24-hour media rule is a commitment, not an aspiration. If a shorter period is technically possible for a given job, we delete sooner. Do not treat our storage as a backup: keep your own master copies.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf, and only as far as needed:

  • Hosting and delivery — Cloudflare serves this site and helps protect it.
  • Email — Google Workspace handles our mailbox, including beta requests.
  • Storage and speech-to-text — vendors for object storage and captioning are still being finalised; they will be listed on the Subprocessors page before the beta opens more widely.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to:

  • comply with a valid court order, subpoena or lawful request;
  • investigate and stop abuse, fraud, or prohibited content such as child sexual abuse material;
  • protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public, or us; or
  • complete a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different policy.

The current provider list, with roles, is kept on the Subprocessors page.

7. Where your data goes

We and our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where data protection law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Our counsel will confirm the final transfer mechanism; the hosting and storage regions will be listed on the Subprocessors page once vendors are final.

8. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for the site and uploads.
  • Access to production systems limited to the people who need it, with individual accounts.
  • Uploads held in object storage that is not publicly listable, addressed by unguessable keys.
  • Short retention windows, so there is less data to lose.
  • Abuse and malware checks before media is processed.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law. More detail is on our Security page.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • Delete your account and data (subject to legal retention duties);
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Port data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing such as marketing email; and
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email hello@arrowlogy.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We aim to respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will not charge you for a normal request.

10. Children

The Service is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We will update this policy as the beta develops and change the date at the top. For material changes we will email beta users. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: hello@arrowlogy.com.
