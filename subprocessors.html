1. What a subprocessor is

A subprocessor is a third-party company that processes personal data on our behalf so we can run the Service — for example, sending our email or serving this website. We stay responsible for what they do on our instructions, and we only use providers we believe are appropriate for the job.

This page is the public list. It is referenced from our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

4. Rules we hold them to

  • They process data only on our documented instructions, and not for their own purposes.
  • They must not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • They must protect data with appropriate technical and organisational measures.
  • They must delete or return data when the arrangement ends.
  • They must tell us about a breach without undue delay.
  • They must flow down equivalent obligations to their own subprocessors.

Where a provider processes data outside your country, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires it. Counsel will confirm the final transfer mechanism.

5. Changes to this list

We will update this page when a provider changes or a pending row is filled in, and change the date at the top. Beta users will be emailed about material changes, including any new provider that touches your media. If you object to a new subprocessor, you can stop using the Service and ask us to delete your account.
