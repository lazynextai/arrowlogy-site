Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Beta — pending counsel review

Some content is banned outright. Arrowlogy is a general-purpose video editor, not a system for medical records, identity documents or regulated data. Uploading medical, health, patient, biometric-identification, government-ID, illegal or confidential third-party content is a serious breach — we block it where we can, and we terminate accounts when we find it.

1. Who this applies to

This Acceptable Use Policy is part of our Terms of Service and applies to everyone who uses Arrowlogy — beta testers, invited users, and anyone who emails us media. It covers what you upload, what you ask our workers to process, the names you give things, and how you behave toward other people and our systems.

2. Hard-banned content

The following categories are prohibited without exception. This is a hard ban, not a request. Do not upload them, do not ask a worker to process them, and do not use Arrowlogy to store, transcode or caption them.

Medical, health and patient content

  • Medical records, clinical notes, referral letters, discharge summaries, prescriptions.
  • Patient information of any kind, including appointment recordings, consultations, and intake videos.
  • Diagnostic imagery — X-rays, scans, ultrasounds, pathology slides, endoscopy or surgery footage.
  • Therapy, counselling or psychiatric session recordings.
  • Any recording that reveals a named or identifiable person’s health condition, treatment, disability or diagnosis.

Biometric identification content

  • Face, voice, fingerprint, iris, palm or gait recordings collected to identify or verify a person.
  • Datasets assembled for training or testing recognition systems.
  • Recordings made to enrol someone in an identity or access system.

Ordinary footage where people simply appear is not the problem; building identity data from footage is. If the purpose of the clip is to identify someone biometrically, it is banned here.

Government identity documents

  • Passports, driving licences, national ID cards, residence permits, visas.
  • Social security, tax or national insurance identifiers, and documents showing them.
  • Onboarding or KYC videos that display identity documents.
  • Any recording of a document used to prove who someone is.

Illegal content

  • Child sexual abuse material, or any content that sexualises a minor.
  • Credible threats of violence, incitement to violence, or content that promotes terrorism.
  • Content facilitating human trafficking, sexual exploitation, or the sale of illegal goods.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, including sexual deepfakes of real people.
  • Anything illegal where you are, where we operate, or where our providers process data.

Content of or involving minors

  • Do not upload footage of children. Minors may not hold accounts, and the Service is for adults 18 and over.
  • If a clip would necessarily include a child, do not upload it — edit it elsewhere.

Someone else’s confidential material

  • Material covered by an NDA, a confidentiality agreement, or an employment duty.
  • Trade secrets, unreleased product footage, internal company recordings.
  • Privileged legal documents or recordings of legal advice.
  • Another person’s private footage or personal recordings, unless you have clear authority to process it.

3. Other prohibited uses

You also must not use Arrowlogy to:

  • Infringe rights — upload content you do not own or license, including music, film clips, broadcast footage or stock media without the right to use it.
  • Harass or defame — target, threaten, dox or defame a person, or produce content designed to humiliate them.
  • Impersonate — pass yourself off as another person, brand or organisation, including with synthetic voice or face content presented as real.
  • Deceive — create disinformation, fake news, fraudulent evidence, or synthetic media intended to mislead about real events or real people.
  • Spam or scam — build phishing, fraud or mass-marketing material.
  • Abuse the platform — attempt to bypass limits, probe or breach security, scrape the Service, run denial-of-service traffic, or reverse engineer our systems.
  • Resell without permission — offer Arrowlogy itself as your own product, or resell beta access.
  • Break export rules — use the Service from a country subject to sanctions, or in violation of export-control law.

4. Rights and permissions

Only upload what you have the right to upload. That means you own it, you have a licence that permits cloud processing, or you have written permission from the person who does. If you are editing for a client, confirm they have cleared the footage, the music and any people appearing in it.

You keep ownership of your content — see section 6 of the Terms of Service. This policy limits what you may put into the Service; it does not take anything away from you.

5. How we enforce it

Enforcement is real and it is not a warning-only system. Depending on what we find and how serious it is, we may:

  • Block an upload before it is stored or processed.
  • Delete media immediately, including rendered exports.
  • Suspend or terminate the account without prior notice.
  • Refuse future invitations and close the beta door to that person.
  • Report to law enforcement where the law requires or permits it, especially for child sexual abuse material.
  • Preserve limited records where needed for an investigation, a legal obligation, or a report to authorities.

We use automated checks on uploads and job metadata, plus manual review when there is a credible report or a clear signal of prohibited content. Automated checks are imperfect and will sometimes flag legitimate material; if that happens, reply to our email and we will look at it.

Beta access is free, so termination means losing access. It does not create a refund obligation, because nothing was paid.

6. Reporting a problem

If you find content on the Service, or someone using it, that breaks this policy, email hello@arrowlogy.com with the subject “Abuse report”. Include what you saw, where, and when. If a child is at immediate risk, contact your local emergency services first.

We treat reports confidentially and will not tell the reported account who reported it.

7. Appeals

If we blocked an upload or suspended your account and you believe it was a mistake, email hello@arrowlogy.com with the subject “Appeal”. Tell us what the content was and why it is allowed. We will review it once and reply. Decisions on child sexual abuse material and clear illegality are final.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@arrowlogy.com.
