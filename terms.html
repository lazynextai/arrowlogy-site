5. Cloud processing

Arrowlogy edits in your browser, but the heavy work happens on our cloud workers. When you upload media, you are sending it to our storage so automated workers can transcode, caption, render, and return results to your browser tab. In plain terms:

  • Your uploads are transferred over encrypted connections and stored in our object storage.
  • Automated workers process them; we do not pass your footage to a human editor to cut for you.
  • We do not use your content to train machine-learning models.

You are responsible for having the right to upload what you upload, and for keeping your own master copies. Do not treat our storage as a backup or a library.
